# Limitations of the ECQC Inequality: A Counterexample in Composite Dimension d = 4

Event: University of Wyoming Science Institute Annual Research Symposium 

Locations: University of Wyoming, USA

Date: April 2026

Presentation Type: Poster Presentation 

Short Description: This poster investigates the limitations of the Extended Complementary Quantum Correlations (ECQC) inequality in composite dimensions, focusing on dimension (d=4). It examines a maximally entangled bipartite quantum state using five mutually unbiased bases (MUBs) and presents a counterexample in which the sum of classical measurement correlations exceeds the total quantum mutual information. The findings highlight potential limitations of the ECQC conjecture in composite dimensions and motivate further research toward generalized ECQC inequality valid for all bipartite quantum states.
